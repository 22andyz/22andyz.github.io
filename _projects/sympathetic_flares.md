---
title: "Sympathetic Flares"
summary: "Detection of sympathy in stellar flare occurrence rates at short wait times using new flare detection algorithm TOFFEE on TESS photometry."
tech: [TOFFEE, TESS]
link: "https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.20311"
---

As the most well-studied star, the Sun has an established pattern of 'sympathetic flares'. That is, while the occurrence of solar flares is mostly stochastic, a small percentage are triggered by the occurrence shortly beforehand. As these flares comprise a small fraction of well-resolved solar activity, this behavior on large samples of other stars has had no precedence until now.

We utilize the new TESS Overlapping Flare Finder and Energy Evaluator (TOFFEE) to process over 62,000 TESS 2-minute cadence photometric lightcurves from over 16,000 stars, detecting over 220,000 flares. Charting the wait times between consecutive flares normalized by flare rates, we find between a 4-9% excess of short wait time flares beyond what is expected from flares that behave like purely poisson-distributed events. This is an indication of that sympathy is far from a solely solar phenomenon and is instead common across most stars in our universe.
